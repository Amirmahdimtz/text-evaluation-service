## Authorization and preflight gate (do not infer consent)

Before any real internal staging, model, GPU, DB, IAM, network, vulnerability scanner or load test, record signed organizational authorization for Infrastructure/SRE, Security/Network, IAM, Data Steward/Legal, AI Lead/SMEs and DBA where applicable. Confirm approved isolated staging and service identity; synthetic/authorized test fixtures, source classification and permitted artifacts; immutable model and tokenizer revisions plus legal/license acceptance; network segmentation and full listener isolation; no-external-egress design and safe observation permissions; resource/timeout/abort limits; evidence retention and private storage; RPO/RTO and approved thresholds. **Not supplied or verified at initiation.**

Execution order after approval: (1) read-only private inventory/provenance preflight, (2) authorize synthetic safe staged runtime and isolation tests, (3) candidate model/structured-output contract, (4) three-domain gold quality with adjudicated labels, (5) protected service-level E2E, (6) network/privacy/negative security, (7) bounded load/soak/failover/restore, (8) independent acceptance review and retest. A failed preflight must stop the sequence. No shared DB migration, auto-download, unreviewed worker activation, unsafe Docker access or production deployment.
